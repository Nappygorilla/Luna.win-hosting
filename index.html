FAQ

Questions,
answered.

These answers describe the intended Luna service model. Publish the final policy pages before taking paid signups.

What runtimes are supported?

The initial service target is Node.js and Python Discord bots. The final runtime matrix should be enforced by the container image selected for each plan, including supported major versions.

What happens if my bot crashes?

Luna is designed to detect an exited process and restart it according to the service restart policy. Crash loops should be rate-limited and surfaced in the panel logs instead of being restarted forever.

Can I use environment variables?

Yes. Secrets should be stored as environment variables and injected into the isolated bot container at runtime rather than committed to your repository.

How do backups work?

Backups should cover persistent bot data and configuration according to the final retention policy. Until that policy is implemented and published, Luna does not promise a specific backup frequency or retention period.

What happens when I cancel?

Cancellation should stop renewal first, then preserve service data only for the published grace period before deletion. The exact billing and data-deletion terms should be published before launch.

How do I get started?

Open the Luna client panel to manage hosted bots. Paid provisioning stays closed until the API, billing, email, agent, storage, and operational controls are fully configured.
